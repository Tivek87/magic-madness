## [0.0.1-alpha] - 2026-09-29

### Added
- **Hold `[G]` Spell Radial Menu & Orbiting Aim Ball**: Hold `G` (under the *Magic Madness* keybind section) to open the radial spell wheel. Move your mouse to guide the glowing aim ball outside the center ring toward your spell, and release `G` to cast.
- **6 Elemental Magic Schools & Pixel-Art Icons**: Added custom square pixel-art school icons for **Heat**, **Air**, **Nature**, **Electric**, **Void**, and **Hydro**.
- **Fireball Spell (Heat — 10m Weighted Arc & 10 Damage)**: Launch a weighted Fireball that curves over 10 blocks, explodes on impact for 10 damage (plus bonus fire burn), ignites blocks, and turns into steam in water.
- **Lightning Spell (Electric — 24m Sky Bolt, 3D Storm Cloud & 16 Damage)**: Lock onto targets up to 24 blocks away to summon an overhead 3D storm cloud and multi-branched lightning bolt that deals 16 impact damage and chains between nearby enemies (up to 5 jumps in the rain).
- **Wind Gust Spell (Air — 10m Gale Cone, Low Damage & Massive Knockback)**: Blast enemies backward and up into the air with a 10-block storm cone (3 damage, huge knockback), reflect incoming arrows and projectiles, douse fire, and cushion your fall in mid-air.
- **In-Game Client & Server Spell Configs**: Customize visual feedback on the client and tune damage, cooldowns, range, and knockback for every spell in the server config menu.
